# Deltaware Admin Guide

Welcome to the Deltaware Admin Dashboard. As an administrator, you have full control over the point of sale (POS) system, inventory, pricing, analytics, and workers.

## 1. Dashboard & Analytics
Upon logging in, you will be greeted by the **Analytics Dashboard**.
- **Key Metrics**: View total revenue, TVA collected, and top performing products at a glance.
- **Date Filtering**: Use the date picker to analyze specific days, weeks, or custom date ranges.
- **Charts**: Visual representations of your sales trends over time.

## 2. Inventory & Products
Navigate to the **Products** tab to manage your inventory.
- **Add Product**: Click "New Product". Enter the Reference Code, Name, Base Cost (Prix Achat), Margins (Gros & Détail), and TVA rate. The system automatically computes your final HT and TTC prices.
- **Stock Updates**: You can manually adjust stock if you receive new shipments or perform an inventory count.

## 3. Worker Management
Navigate to the **Workers** tab to control your staff's access.
- **Create Worker**: You can invite new workers via email and set temporary passwords.
- **Permissions Matrix**: Toggle specific permissions for each worker (e.g., `manage_stock`, `make_sales`, `view_analytics`). Workers will only see the features you grant them access to.

## 4. Store Settings
Navigate to the **Settings** tab.
- **Identity**: Update the store name, address, and logo. These will appear on all printed PDFs.
- **Global Taxes**: Define default TVA rates and Timbre Fiscal parameters.

## Troubleshooting
If you encounter missing data, ensure your workers are properly assigned to the correct store and that they have synchronized their latest sales.
