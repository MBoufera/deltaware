# Deltaware Worker Guide

Welcome to the Deltaware Point of Sale (POS). This guide will help you process sales and manage daily store operations.

## 1. Starting a Sale
1. Navigate to the **Point of Sale** screen.
2. Select the **Sale Type**: 
   - `Détail`: Regular walk-in customers.
   - `Gros`: Wholesale clients (applies wholesale pricing margins).
   - `Bon de Livraison` or `Facture`: Official documents that require selecting a registered Client.
3. If applicable, click **Select Client** and choose from the registry.

## 2. Managing the Cart
- **Search**: Type a product name or Reference Code into the search bar.
- **Add**: Tap a product card to add it to the cart.
- **Quantity**: Use the `+` and `-` buttons in the cart panel to adjust quantities.
- **Timbre Fiscal**: If the customer requires an official facture, toggle the **Timbre Fiscal (1%)** switch at the bottom of the cart. The total TTC will update automatically.

## 3. Checkout and Printing
1. Review the Grand Total TTC.
2. Click **Valider la Vente** (Confirm Sale).
3. The system will automatically generate a highly formatted A4 or Receipt-sized PDF.
4. The PDF viewer will open. Click the **Print** icon to send it directly to your thermal printer or A4 laser printer.

## 4. Document History
If you need to reprint a receipt or view past sales, navigate to the **Document History** tab. You can filter past sales by date or receipt number and regenerate the PDF instantly.
